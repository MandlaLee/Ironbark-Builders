What we do

Services

One crew, one point of contact, from the first site walk to the final punch list. We self-perform framing and finish carpentry and manage every trade on your job.

Custom Home Building

Ground-up construction on mountain lots, infill lots, and steep-grade sites. Architect coordination, permitting, and full build management.

Additions & Second Stories

Bump-outs, primary suites, garages, and full second-story additions engineered to tie cleanly into your existing structure.

Kitchen & Bath Remodeling

Layout changes, cabinetry, tile, stone, and fixture packages. Dust barriers and daily cleanup so you can stay in your home.

Decks & Outdoor Living

Timber-framed decks, screened porches, pergolas, and outdoor kitchens — built for WNC weather with proper footings and drainage.

Light Commercial Build-Outs

Tenant improvements, offices, studios, and retail spaces. ADA compliance, code coordination, and after-hours scheduling available.

Repairs & Structural Work

Rot repair, beam and joist replacement, foundation piers, water intrusion, and post-storm damage. Free assessments for storm claims.

Who we are

Two decades of WNC framing and finish

Ironbark Builders started in 2009 as a two-man framing crew working out of a single truck in West Asheville. Sixteen years later we run four crews and a full-time project management team — but the standard hasn't moved.

Owner Marcus Delaney is on every job site. Not a salesperson, not a call center. You get a written scope, a fixed schedule, weekly photo updates, and a crew that shows up when they say they will.
